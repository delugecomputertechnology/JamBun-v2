How JamBun Works

JamBun is a free friendship quiz. You answer ten questions about yourself, share your link, and your friends try to guess your answers. Everyone gets a score and a place on the leaderboard. JamBun is made only for fun. A low score does not mean someone is a bad friend, so please do not take the results seriously.

How to play

  1. Create: tap "Find Your Fake Friend", type a nickname (not your full real name), and pick your answers to ten picture questions.
  2. Choose a reward: pick the animation that friends will see if they score well.
  3. Share: copy your link or use the Share button to send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or any app.
  4. Guess: your friends open the link, enter a nickname, and guess your answers. After each guess they see if they were right.
  5. Compare: check the leaderboard to see who knows you best.

Scoring

Each correct guess is one point, out of ten. Score 7 or more and you are a "Real friend" who unlocks the reward. Score below 7 and you are marked OUT, which is just a playful label. You can always create your own quiz and challenge your friends back.

FAQ

Is JamBun free?

Yes. There is no sign up and no payment. You only type a nickname.

Can I edit my quiz after saving it?

No. If you want different answers, create a new quiz and share the new link.

How can I delete my quiz or score?

Email us from the Contact page with your quiz link or nickname.

Is it for children?

JamBun is not meant for children under 13. Please never put personal details in your nickname. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
